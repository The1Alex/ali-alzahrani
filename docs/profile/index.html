Professional profile

I have never faced an issue without providing a solution.

That is how I describe my own approach, and it is a high bar to set in public. The rest of this site is the evidence.

My journey

I began my path on a Saudi Aramco scholarship: a preparatory programme in Saudi Arabia, then computer science at the University of Nottingham in the UK and at King Fahd University of Petroleum & Minerals. Along the way I joined leadership programmes in Leeds and Newcastle. I went on to complete a BSc in Information Technology and Computing at the Arab Open University.

My working life started in the hardest possible classroom for an IT person: a construction contractor working for Aramco. As a Project Engineer I directed IT and construction contractors to stand up nine remote sites. Within a year I was Head of IT, responsible for 699 users, a migration to Exchange Online and regaining control of more than 1,000 devices.

In 2017 I joined Vallourec, an industrial manufacturer, as an IT Specialist on the shop floor. That is where I learned how production really runs. I built a QR-code processing application, kept product tracking systems accurate, and was part of the crisis cell during COVID-19. I then spent three years as Infrastructure Expert and Project Manager, mapping the plant's network, leading the Aramco Cyber Security Certification and moving identity and devices to the Microsoft cloud.

Since January 2024 I have been Middle East IT Leader, working across four international sites as the link between the business and our global Business Applications, Infrastructure, Endpoint and Security functions. I also lead regional AI adoption, and that has become central to how I think about the next stage of my career.

Contribution

What I bring to an organisation

  • A bridge between the business and IT

    I listen to what site leaders need, turn it into one clear plan, and work with global teams to deliver it.

  • Delivery from first requirement to handover

    Requirements, design, build, testing, cutover and handover to support, with training and follow-up afterwards.

  • AI adoption that people actually use

    Practical enablement, use-case discovery and working applications, with governance in view from the start.

  • Stability in demanding environments

    I choose the stable, long-term solution over the fastest one. Factories, Aramco requirements and ISO standards taught me why.

  • Commercial discipline

    Vendor selection, negotiation and consolidation, in partnership with Procurement and Finance.

  • Order where there was none

    Procedures, standards, documentation and registers that outlast the project and the person who wrote them.

How I work

  • People know they can count on me. When something important has to get done, managers come to me.
  • I lead with logic and common sense, and I always think about people too.
  • I break big projects into small, clear steps. Some days I work with teams in different time zones, and some days I work alone on plans, tests and procedures.
  • I don't stop at delivery. I train people on the solutions I deliver, and I follow up with them.
