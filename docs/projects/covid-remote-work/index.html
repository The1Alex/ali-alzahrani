Keeping the business running through COVID-19

With one technician, moved seven departments and about 100 people to remote work as part of the crisis cell.

My role
Crisis cell member
Status
Completed; recognised with the Excellence Leader Award, 2020
Position
IT Specialist

1The challenge

The pandemic meant people could no longer work from the plant and office as usual, and the business still had to run. There were two of us to do the work.

2My responsibility

I was part of the crisis cell, and with one IT technician I carried out the move to remote work.

3My reasoning and decisions

  • Prepare the whole working setup for each person: laptop, VPN, Teams training and a home office that worked.
  • Train people on Teams, so that the change worked in practice and not only on paper.

4The solution

We prepared laptops and VPN access, trained people on Teams and set up remote offices for about 100 people across seven departments.

5The result

The business kept running. I received the Excellence Leader Award in 2020 for performance through pandemic operations.

6Skills demonstrated

  • VPN enablement
  • Microsoft Teams
  • User training
  • Business continuity
  • Office and remote-work adaptability
  • Accountability
