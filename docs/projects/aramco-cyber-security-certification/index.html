Aramco Cyber Security Certification

Took the company through certification required by a key customer, ahead of a three-month deadline, then went further than asked and secured every connected device.

My role
Principal lead
Status
Completed ahead of schedule, followed by a one-year device programme
Position
Infrastructure Expert & Project Manager

1The challenge

A key customer required us to certify against its cybersecurity controls within three months, and failure could have stopped the business in the long term. Many technical safeguards were already in place. The harder gaps were in procedures, training and proving that every user had acknowledged the policy.

2My responsibility

I was the principal lead for the certification, working with an authorised third party, departmental managers and the factory director.

3My reasoning and decisions

  • Explain the business stakes to each department's management first, and secure the factory director's support.
  • Design the training myself around the customer's actual requirements, and deliver it across shifts so nobody was missed.
  • Automate policy acknowledgement with Microsoft automation and keep an auditable Excel record.
  • Go beyond the requirement: nobody asked us to inspect every PC and laptop, but I took the lead in doing so.

4The solution

I closed the control gaps with the authorised third party, trained employees shift by shift, and sent each attendee the policy with an automated acknowledgement step. A twelve-month programme then followed to secure every device connected to the Saudi network.

5The result

Certification achieved for Vallourec Saudi Arabia ahead of the expected schedule, then a completed year-long connected-device security programme. The certification belongs to the company, not to me personally.
