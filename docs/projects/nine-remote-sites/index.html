Nine remote sites for more than 500 users

Directed IT and construction contractors to stand up nine remote project sites, and resolved 79% of reported issues remotely.

My role
Directed
Status
Completed on time and within budget
Position
Project Engineer

1The challenge

An Aramco contractor needed full infrastructure and IT assets at nine remote project sites, keeping more than 500 users connected.

2My responsibility

I directed the IT and construction contractors, coordinated nine IT support and technical teams, and led vendor negotiation.

3My reasoning and decisions

  • Resolve as much as possible remotely at first line, to avoid sending people to site.
  • Isolate two site networks, to strengthen security and keep daily operations going.
  • Audit sites every two to three months so that standards held after setup.

4The solution

Each site received full infrastructure and IT assets. I ran remote services through the nine coordinated teams, negotiated laptops, servers and accessories, and worked with clients on the design of their site systems.

5The result

Nine sites delivered for more than 500 users, on time and within budget, with 79% of reported technical issues resolved remotely.
