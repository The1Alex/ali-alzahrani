Digitalising a plant, from paper to live data

Moved shop-floor meetings, quality records, filing and procurement away from paper, and gave production management visibility of cycle times and shutdowns.

My role
Directed; third-party application
Status
Implemented, and expanding
Position
Middle East IT Leader

1The challenge

Daily shop-floor meetings ran at three levels, from the floor to supervisors to department managers, and escalation relied on paper. Departments still used physical files, quality inspections were recorded on paper, and production management had limited visibility of where time was being lost.

2My responsibility

The plant director asked me to find a solution for digitalising the shop-floor process. I gathered the requirements from each department, found the third-party provider who built the web application, supplied the hardware, and direct the wider digitalisation programme.

3My reasoning and decisions

  • Meet the stakeholders in each department before choosing anything, so that the process reflected what they expected.
  • Bring a touchscreen experience onto the floor. Large screens were my proposal, chosen to suit the environment and the people using it.
  • Pilot in three buildings before asking the business to commit.
  • Keep IT requests traceable by logging each one raised on the floor in our global ticketing system, so that it was followed through to closure.

4The solution

Shop-floor teams report on large touchscreens, with photos and named owners. I also moved departments from physical filing to SharePoint with automated workflows and digital signatures, introduced digital quality log sheets and digital procurement, and introduced cycle-time monitoring and shutdown analysis.

5The result

Escalation became clearer, with email notifications and photo-based reporting, and paper use fell. After the pilot, the solution was adopted across the plant and grew to cover safety, maintenance, repair and IT requests. It was also demonstrated to process managers from other group entities, and production management gained visibility it previously lacked.
