The machine that disappeared

A production application lost contact with a running machine. The first fix failed, so I went beyond my scope and found the cause with research and elimination.

My role
Investigated hands-on
Status
Resolved
Position
Middle East IT Leader

1The challenge

A production application could no longer communicate with a machine, even though the machine itself was running. The server side looked fine. Replacing the cable, the obvious first fix for equipment in heat and dust, did not solve it.

2My responsibility

The fault sat on the automation side, outside my scope and my comfort zone. I took it on anyway.

3My reasoning and decisions

  • Treat the failed first attempt as information, and reassess.
  • Eliminate each link in turn until the interface device between the machine and the switch was the only candidate left.
  • Use online research and AI tools together to find the manual and the correct configuration.
  • Finish with a lessons-learned session, so the fix did not live only in my head.

4The solution

I tested the device away from the line, checked its settings and its physical configuration, researched the correct setup, and restored communication after several attempts.

5The result

The machine showed up in the application again. I shared the standard with automation, maintenance and operational colleagues in a lessons-learned session. Why the setting changed in the first place remains unknown.

  • Research and analysis
  • Persistence

  • Explaining complex problems clearly
