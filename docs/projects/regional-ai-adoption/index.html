Regional AI adoption

Taught around 300 employees to use Microsoft Copilot on their real work, then turned the best ideas from the business into working applications.

My role
Led
Status
Delivered, adoption continuing
Position
Middle East IT Leader

1The challenge

AI tools only create value when people outside IT know how to use them on everyday tasks. Departments needed practical capability, and the business needed to know which ideas were worth building.

2My responsibility

I led AI adoption across the region, including the training, the use-case initiative and delivery of the resulting applications.

3My reasoning and decisions

  • Train on everyday tasks in each department rather than on the technology in the abstract.
  • Treat the training as the start of discovery, by collecting use cases from the people doing the work.
  • Carry the best findings through to working applications, so that ideas did not stop at a list.
  • Keep governance in view, informed by the ISO 42001 standard for AI management.

4The solution

I delivered Microsoft Copilot training across multiple departments, then established an initiative to identify high-value AI use cases across the business and delivered working applications from the findings.

5The result

Around 300 employees trained across departments, and working applications delivered from the use-case initiative.
