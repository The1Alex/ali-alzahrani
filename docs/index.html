How I show up

What people can count on, with the evidence.

  1. Resourceful

    I investigate, learn what I don't yet know and connect ideas from other fields. When a production machine dropped off its application, I went past the edge of my own scope, into the automation hardware, and researched my way to the answer.

    The machine that disappeared

  2. Accountable

    I own the outcome, including the part after go-live. During COVID, one technician and I moved seven departments and about 100 people to remote work, and the business kept running.

    The COVID crisis cell
